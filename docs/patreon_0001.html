That's not creating. That's plumbing.

All in one window. Every artifact instantly reusable by every other function. Out of the box, with best-practice settings already dialed in.

Post 1 — Images: a full darkroom, not a single button

Generate, edit, restore, compose, and lock a face — with the best open image models, all in one tab.

📷 IMAGE — images_hero_grid.png — a 3×2 grid: photoreal portrait, cinematic scene, anime, product shot, restored old photo, colorized B&W. Shows range.

Generate with the models that matter — your pick

  • FLUX.2, FLUX.1 & FLUX.1 Krea (photoreal aesthetic), Z-Image, Ernie, Qwen-Image, SD3 — native (diffusers), no ComfyUI.
  • Auto-detection reads the model header and configures components for you; dropdowns are color-coded by VRAM (green/yellow/red) so you never guess.
  • Live per-step preview — watch the image form.

Edit anything, in place

  • Inpaint / Outpaint natively (FLUX.1 Fill, FLUX.2 Klein) — extend a canvas or repaint a region.
  • Kontext instruction edit — tell it what to change in words.
  • Compose 2+ photos without cut-and-paste seams (multi-reference / stitch, optional masks).
  • Prompt Builder (JSON) and an invisible "structure my prompt" checkbox that quietly turns free text into a clean blueprint before generating.

Faces & restoration — the details people notice

  • PuLID face adapter: lock a person's identity from photos. Upload one or several shots — it averages them for a rock-solid likeness — with advanced controls (identity strength, when to inject, prompt-adherence).
  • Face-swap (InsightFace) on any image.
  • Restore damaged photos and colorize black-and-white with the most advanced models.
  • Upscale / super-resolution, segmentation (SAM2 + GroundingDINO) for precise selections.
  • Storyboard multiple scenes as timed keyframes.

Post 2 — Video: from one image to any length, with audio

Text-to-video, image-to-video, editing, character replacement, restoration — powered by LTX-2.3, native.

🎬 VIDEO — video_hero_reel.mp4 — a 45s highlight reel: t2v shot, i2v from a still, a character swap, a style change (real→anime), an upscaled clip. Fast cuts, music.

Create

  • Text-to-video and image-to-video (your generated image becomes the first frame automatically).
  • Audio in the generation: sing / lip-sync to real audio, text-to-audio, HDR/EXR output.
  • Unlimited length: a memory-bounded autoregressive chain extends clips to any duration — with crash-resume and anti-drift face/identity re-anchoring.
  • Fast / Pro / HQ modes; new TeaCache speed toggle for many-step renders.
  • Keyframe interpolation and multi-scene storyboard → one continuous video, no editing.

Edit & direct

  • Copy the motion of any video (ControlNet: canny / depth / pose).
  • Instruction editing ("make it snowy winter"), masked inpaint where the mask tracks the object across frames (SAM2), style transfer (real↔anime), ReTake a section, lip-sync a talking head.
  • Character replacement: swap the person in a clip while keeping the scene, motion and original audio intact — one replacement image is enough.
  • MultiTalk lip-sync to real audio, even for long videos.

Finish & restore

  • Super-resolution (SeedVR2, FlashVSR), frame interpolation for higher FPS (RIFE), per-frame face restoration.
  • Restore & colorize old film with the latest models.
  • Real per-step video preview and a credible progress bar.

FAQ

Is it really all local?

Yes — 100% on your own GPU. No cloud calls at runtime, no accounts, your files never leave your machine. (An optional token is only ever used for a one-time download of gated model weights.)

Do I need to know ComfyUI or node graphs?

No. That's the whole point. Clean GUI, best-practice defaults, one button. If you've ever rage-quit a 40-node graph, this is for you.

What GPU do I need?

It auto-tunes for cards from 8 GB to 128 GB (dynamic VRAM management, block-swap, quantized model options). More VRAM = faster and higher-res, but small cards are supported.

What's the difference between the tiers?

Free and Supporter ($5) include a watermark; Creator ($14) removes it; Unlimited ($19) removes content restrictions; Studio ($25) adds training, model import, AI Film Studio and 3D/mocap. All are local.

Can I train a character with just a photo?

Yes. The app generates a coherent dataset (images, then videos) from your photo(s) and trains image & video LoRAs automatically.

Can I use my own models?

Yes, on the Studio tier — import checkpoints and fine-tunes and point the app at your paths.

How often is it updated?

Frequently — expect new features, models and architectures on a roughly weekly cadence. This is an actively growing platform, not a frozen release.

Can other tools / agents drive it?

Yes — a local API plus agent packs (Hermes, OpenClaw, Claude Code, Antigravity, Codex) expose every function programmatically.
